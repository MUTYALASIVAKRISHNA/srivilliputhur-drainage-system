Engineering Design

11-Phase Academic Prototype Methodology

A systematic multi-tier framework for integrating heterogeneous public data, synthetic machine learning models, GIS mapping, and civic workflows.

1

Phase 1: Collect & Organize Public City Information

Aggregated accessible government and demographic records: 33 official wards from the Commissioner of Municipal Administration, 2011 Census populations, Wikipedia geographical profiles, and historical sanitation data.

2

Phase 2: Strict Data Segregation (Layer 1 vs Layer 2)

Established an uncompromising architectural boundary between real/public evidence (Layer 1: 12 incidents, 1 tender drain, 33 wards, 4 rainfall portals) and synthetic machine learning records (Layer 2: 50 rows). Synthetic data is never masqueraded as physical municipal sensors.

3

Phase 3: Validate Synthetic ML Dataset & Investigate Leakage

Conducted exploratory data analysis on the 50 synthetic records. Identified zero HIGH target instances (32 LOW, 18 MEDIUM). Quantified target leakage on Blockage_Percent (\(r=0.8508\)), proving that blockage acts as a trivial proxy rather than an upstream causal factor.

4

Phase 4: Train Machine Learning Prototypes

Trained two supervised classification algorithms: Logistic Regression (linear baseline) and Random Forest Classifier (ensemble non-linear). Developed a leakage-free Causal Pipeline utilizing pre-blockage features: antecedent rainfall, plastic score, channel width/depth, water head, and cleaning recency.

5

Phase 5: Model Evaluation & Cross-Validation

Evaluated models using Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation, test accuracy, precision, recall, F1-scores, and confusion matrices. Extracted Gini feature importances showing water depth (22.3%) and drain width (16.2%) as primary non-leaking predictors.

6

Phase 6: Analyze Rainfall Separately

Distinguished Virudhunagar District regional statistics (NWDP/IMD) from local town events. Prevented misleading claims of nonexistent automated station gauges in Srivilliputhur.

7

Phase 7: Analyze Historical Incidents Separately

Cataloged 12 flood events with verbatim reported causes. Avoided the false assumption that all past floods were caused by drainage choking (e.g., differentiating irrigation tank surplus releases from silt blockage).

8

Phase 8: Analyze Drainage & Plastic Waste Evidence

Integrated findings from the SFD Lite Report (31 TPD waste generation, 101.1 km open drains) and local municipal enforcement fines along Netaji Road. Established the standardized 4-tier visual observation rubric (NONE, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH).

9

Phase 9: Interactive GIS Risk Mapping

Engineered an interactive Leaflet/OpenStreetMap interface visualizing the 50 synthetic prototype points, verified tender drain infrastructure in Ward 32, and documented flood landmarks with custom risk badges and popup data.

10

Phase 10: Citizen Reporting & Maintenance Workflow

Built end-to-end municipal grievance tracking: citizen ticket generation (SVP-REP-2026-XXXX), tracking portal, and sanitation crew dispatch logs with de-silting and waste removal records.

11

Phase 11: Future Physical Field Validation

Defined 25 planned field survey points across key town bottlenecks (Bus Stand, Farmers Market, Car Streets). Outlined the protocol for transitioning planned points into verified physical observations once on-site fieldwork is conducted.
