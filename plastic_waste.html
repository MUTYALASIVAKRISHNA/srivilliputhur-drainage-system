Standardized Field Observation Scoring Rubric

FIELD_RUBRIC

Per the project survey template, plastic levels are classified visually by field surveyors across 4 rigorous tiers:

Score 0 — NONE
No visible plastic items or synthetic packaging observed in the channel segment.
Score 1 — LOW
Isolated items; less than 25% of visible drain channel cross-section/area affected.
Score 2 — MEDIUM
Noticeable accumulation; between 25% and 50% of channel surface obstructed.
Score 3 — HIGH
Extensive accumulation; greater than 50% of drain choked by single-use plastics and packaging.
