Causal Integrity Rule

Do NOT automatically classify every historical flood as a drainage blockage. Many events were caused by Western Ghats cloudbursts, kanmai (irrigation tank) breaches, or full reservoir surplus discharges. If the news source does not state drainage blockage, the cause is recorded strictly as stated or marked "Not Available / Not Stated".
