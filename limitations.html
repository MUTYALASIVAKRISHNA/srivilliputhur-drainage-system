Data Limitations & Research Boundary Conditions

In accordance with engineering research integrity standards, the following 9 boundary constraints are explicitly documented. These reflect expected real-world data collection realities, not software defects.

1

Incomplete 33-Ward Municipal Drain GIS Inventory

No official, downloadable GIS shapefile or complete 33-ward physical drain asset registry exists in open public repositories. Only one specific public tender record (Ward 32, Bharathi Nagar 3rd St Road, 120m) and municipal aggregates (101.1 km total network) could be independently verified.

2

Absence of Drain-Level Sensor Blockage Observations

Srivilliputhur Municipality does not currently operate IoT water-level gauges or ultrasonic blockage telemetry inside roadside drains. Blockage percentages in real drains require primary visual inspection.

3

Unquantified Drain-by-Drain Plastic Accumulation

While solid waste generation is documented at the municipal aggregate scale (31 TPD) and anti-littering enforcement fines are recorded in Ward 19, empirical kilogram masses of plastic inside individual open channels have not been measured.

4

Inaccessible Public Maintenance Logs

Historical de-silting schedules and daily municipal sanitation truck dispatches are maintained as internal local body paper records and are not published online. The system's maintenance module operates as a prototype workflow.

5

Unverified Local Station Daily Rainfall Time-Series

National portals (NWDP/IMD) provide Virudhunagar District daily rainfall CSV datasets, but a complete, continuous multi-year daily rain gauge series specific to Srivilliputhur town station was not publicly accessible.

6

Incomplete Ward GIS Boundaries & Street Crosswalk

Although a visual municipal ward map exists, vector GIS polygon shapefiles defining exact boundary coordinates for all 33 wards and the complete 143-street crosswalk could not be verified in open government portals.

7

Non-Drainage Causes in Historical Flood Events

Historical waterlogging incidents cannot automatically be assumed to be caused by drain blockage. Several documented events (e.g. 2021 & 2023) were driven by irrigation tank (kanmai) surplus overflows and Western Ghats cloudburst torrents rather than municipal channel clogging.

8

Synthetic Machine Learning Dataset (50 Rows)

The ML classification models are trained on a 50-row synthetic dataset created solely for academic demonstration. The dataset contains 32 LOW and 18 MEDIUM instances with zero HIGH examples. Consequently, the model cannot legitimately classify HIGH risk without on-site data.

9

Prototype Status — Not for Live Municipal Deployment

This system is an academic engineering capstone prototype. It has not undergone formal civil engineering calibration, hydrodynamic modeling (SWMM), or municipal regulatory validation.
