Rainfall & Meteorological Information

Precipitation drives runoff into urban drainage. The interface strictly separates District-Level rainfall from localized Srivilliputhur reports.

Meteorological Data Rule

Virudhunagar District rainfall data must NEVER be labeled as "Srivilliputhur Station Rainfall". While national portals (IMD/NWDP) provide district-level datasets, a complete verified municipal station daily time-series has not been published in accessible open data.
